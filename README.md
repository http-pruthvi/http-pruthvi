## 📖 Prologue

Hi, I'm **Pruthviraj** — most people just call me Pruthvi.

Somewhere between debugging my first `for` loop and figuring out why my Flutter app kept crashing on Android 12, I realized I didn't just want to *use* software — I wanted to understand what made it think. That curiosity is the thread running through everything below: a CS degree, an internship, a growing shelf of certificates, and a habit of shipping small, slightly-too-ambitious side projects at 1 AM.

This README is that story, told chapter by chapter.

---

## Chapter 1 — Where It Started

> *North Maharashtra Knowledge City, 2023.*

I walked into my B.Tech in Computer Science with a vague sense that I liked building things, and no idea I'd end up caring this much about how machines learn. Four years in (2023–2027), that's changed — chess strategy, competitive problem-solving, and a slowly-forming obsession with AI/ML all found their way into the story around the same time.

## Chapter 2 — The First Real Job

> *UNIKODEX, Jul 2025 → Present*

This is where theory started meeting deadlines. As a **Flutter Developer Intern**, I stopped just learning frameworks and started shipping in them:

- 📱 Building cross-platform mobile apps that real users actually open
- 🎨 Developing responsive UI components under real design constraints
- 🔌 Integrating REST APIs and learning what happens when the docs lie to you

It's the chapter where "I know Flutter" turned into "I've shipped in Flutter" — and those are very different sentences.

## Chapter 5 — Side Quests (a.k.a. Projects)

Every chapter needs a few battles fought outside the main plot. These are mine:

**🌿 AI Plant Disease Detection**
The one where I taught a CNN to spot sick leaves before a farmer could. `Python` `TensorFlow` — landed at **94% accuracy**.

**🤖 ASTRA — Smart AI Assistant**
A voice-controlled assistant I built because typing felt too slow for the ideas I was having. `Python` `NLP` — **85% intent-recognition accuracy**.

**💘 ItoBound — Intelligent Dating App**
Turns out matchmaking is just a recommendation problem in disguise. `Flutter` `Machine Learning` — an AI-powered compatibility engine.

**♟️ Quantum Chess (AI-Enhanced)**
Chess was my first strategy game; this was my attempt to teach a machine to play it with taste. `C++` `AI` — a custom heuristic evaluation engine.
